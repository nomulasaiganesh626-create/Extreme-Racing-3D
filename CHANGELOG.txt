VERSION 0.3.0 — 2026-11-04
- Added reusable procedural vehicle model builder in scripts/car_model.gd.
- Replaced simple box cars with four distinct tapered coupe/hypercar silhouettes.
- Batched body parts by material and used MultiMesh for tyres, alloy rims and hubs.
- Improved garage three-quarter preview and added a display plinth.
- Added moving AI traffic recycling during races.
- Added editable LAN host IP field and connection status callbacks.
- Added upgrade MAXED state and RESET TUNING control.
- Disabled real-time shadows in the race and garage preview for mobile performance.

Known limitations
- No Godot runtime or Android export build was available in the build environment.
- The car models are generated low-poly prototypes, not photorealistic production assets.
- Multiplayer remains experimental LAN transform synchronization, not internet matchmaking.
